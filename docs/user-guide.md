# Using PostNook

PostNook is a writing space in your browser's side panel. You write and format a post, copy it, then paste it into LinkedIn and publish it yourself. PostNook never opens, reads or posts to LinkedIn.

## Open it

Select the PostNook icon in the Chrome or Edge toolbar. The panel opens beside the page you are on, and that page is not read. Your drafts are the same whichever site you have open.

## Write

- **Create first post** starts your first draft; **New draft** (+) starts another.
- Type in the editor. Enter starts a new paragraph, Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- The toolbar has bold, italic, bullet and numbered lists, undo, redo, clear formatting and **Paste as Markdown**. Ctrl+B / Ctrl+I (Cmd on Mac) also work.
- Pasting from Word, Google Docs, ChatGPT or a web page keeps bold, italic and lists and removes everything else. Scripts and styles in pasted content are dropped and never run.
- Name a draft with **Draft title**. Open **Drafts** to switch; the newest appear first.

## Saving

Drafts save automatically on this device while you type. "Saved on this device" means the save finished. If you see **Save failed**, **Storage unavailable** or **Newer version found**, your text is still in the editor; see [support](support.md).

## Preview and copy

**Preview** shows the exact text that Copy Post will put on your clipboard, as a desktop or mobile card. **Copy Post** copies it; then paste it into LinkedIn and publish.

What the preview cannot promise:

- LinkedIn may render the pasted post differently: it may trim blank lines or indentation, and it decides where "see more" cuts the post. PostNook makes no compatibility guarantee.
- Lists are copied as text markers (`•`, `1.`), not as LinkedIn formatting.
- The character count is an **estimate**. LinkedIn states a 3,000-character limit for posts but not how it counts. PostNook counts UTF-16 code units, so some emoji, accented letters built from combining marks and Unicode-styled letters count as two. You can still save and copy a post over the estimate.

## Plain text and Unicode styling

**Plain text** (the default) copies your words without bold or italic, which is the most readable and searchable choice.

**Unicode styling** replaces bold and italic Latin letters with look-alike mathematical characters. Before you first use it, PostNook explains the trade-offs:

- Screen readers may read the letters one by one, read their names, or skip them.
- Search, including LinkedIn search, may not find styled words.
- Some devices and fonts may show boxes or plain letters.
- Accented letters, non-Latin scripts, URLs, hashtags and mentions are never styled.

Your draft itself is never changed by the output mode; you can switch back at any time.

## Appearance

Open **Settings** to choose System (the default), Light or Dark. Your choice is remembered.

## Keep a copy of important posts

Drafts live only in this browser profile on this device. There is no account, sync or backup in v0.1. **Uninstalling PostNook, clearing site data or extension storage, or losing the browser profile deletes your drafts permanently.** Copy important posts somewhere else. Export and backup are planned for a later version (v0.2 in the product plan) and are not available yet.
